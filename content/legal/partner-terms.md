# Partner Terms

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

These terms apply to businesses that provide plant hire or site services to
customers through aggregates.store (the "Platform") as a partner ("you",
"the Partner"). The Platform is operated by Aggregated Aggregates, a
division of Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us"). They are accepted in the
partner portal by an authorised person on the Partner's behalf, and apply
together with any written partner agreement we sign with you.

## 1. Our roles

- We find customers, quote them, take their payment, send jobs to partners
  and keep the record of each job.
- You carry out each job you accept, with your own machines, vehicles,
  operators and crew, as an independent business. Nothing in these terms
  makes you our employee, agent or joint venturer.
- We earn a fee for arranging each job, which we add to your quoted amount
  when we quote the customer. Your payout for a job is the amount shown to
  you in the job offer.

## 2. Joining and staying active

Before you receive offers, and while you remain a partner, you must keep
the following current and give us copies on request:

- company registration and tax-compliance details;
- operator certificates of competence for each machine type, and
  professional driving permits (PrDP) where the law requires them;
- public-liability insurance, and insurance for your machines and
  vehicles;
- roadworthy and licensed vehicles, and machines that are maintained and
  safe to operate;
- a list of your machines and the provinces you work in; and
- a bank confirmation letter for payouts.

Tell us straight away if anything changes — for example, if insurance
lapses, a machine is out of service, or an operator's certificate expires.

## 3. Rates and quotes

- Rates you give us must be in writing, and you must honour them for the
  period stated. We may use written rate cards from several partners to
  publish a price for a machine or service in a province.
- When we ask you to quote a specific job, your written quote must cover
  everything the customer needs for that job (including transport to site)
  unless it says otherwise.

## 4. Offers

- Job offers appear in the partner portal and by email. Every offer is for
  a job the customer has already paid for.
- An offer shows the job, dates, site and your payout. You have **30
  minutes** to accept or decline; after that it goes to the next partner.
- Only accept a job if you have a suitable machine and crew free for the
  full dates. Keep your availability calendar in the portal up to date.
- Accepting an offer is a commitment to do the job as described. If you
  can't do a job you've accepted, tell us immediately through the job's
  messages so we can arrange a replacement.

## 5. Doing the work

- Arrive on the agreed date and time with the machine described, a
  competent operator, fuel and personal protective equipment (for wet
  hire).
- Start the job by entering the customer's 6-digit arrival code in the
  portal. Don't start work without it.
- Work safely and lawfully, including under the Occupational Health and
  Safety Act 85 of 1993 and the Construction Regulations, 2014, and follow
  the site's safety rules. You may refuse work you reasonably consider
  unsafe; tell us straight away.
- Record each day's work on a job card in the portal — hours, hour-meter
  readings or loads — accurately.
- Dispose of rubble and waste only at licensed sites, and keep the tip or
  weighbridge slips.

## 6. Customers and communication

- Communicate with customers through the job's messages in the portal.
  Phone numbers, email addresses and links are removed from messages
  automatically, and we may review messages where that happens or where
  there is a dispute.
- We don't share customers' contact details with you, and you agree not to
  ask customers for them or give yours for the purpose of arranging work
  outside the Platform.

## 7. Not going around the Platform

For **12 months** after we introduce you to a customer, you agree not to
accept work from that customer for plant hire or site services except
through the Platform, unless we agree in writing. This protects the work
we put into finding customers and the protections the Platform gives both
sides. We review possible breaches with you before taking any action.

## 8. Sign-off, disputes and payouts

- When the customer signs off the job, a **48-hour dispute window** starts.
  If no dispute is raised in that time, your payout becomes due and we pay
  it by EFT to your confirmed bank account, normally within 5 business
  days.
- Either you or the customer can raise a dispute while the job is in
  progress or within the dispute window. Your payout is held while a
  dispute is open. We decide disputes fairly on the job cards, messages
  and anything either side provides, and may pay you in full, in part, or
  not at all where the work wasn't done as agreed.
- Payouts are made only to a bank account in the Partner's name, confirmed
  by a bank letter.

## 9. Responsibility and insurance

You are responsible for your machines, vehicles, operators and crew, and
for loss or damage they cause while carrying out a job. You agree to keep
adequate insurance for this, and to compensate us for claims made against
us because of your work, except to the extent the claim is caused by us.

## 10. Your information

We use your business and contact details to run the partnership, send you
offers and pay you, as set out in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).
We don't share your contact details with customers. Your name is shown to a
customer once you accept their job.

## 11. Ratings, suspension and ending the partnership

- We keep a record of your acceptance rate, disputes and customer ratings,
  and use it to decide which partner is offered a job first.
- We may suspend offers to you, or end the partnership, if you breach these
  terms, your documents lapse, or customers repeatedly raise justified
  disputes. We will tell you why, and give you a chance to respond unless
  the issue is serious (for example, a safety incident or fraud).
- You can end the partnership by telling us in writing. Jobs you've already
  accepted must still be completed, and payouts due to you will still be
  paid.

## 12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We'll ask you to accept the updated terms in the
portal before you accept further offers; jobs already accepted continue
under the terms in place when you accepted them.

## 13. Governing law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
Questions about them can be sent to legal@aggregates.store.
