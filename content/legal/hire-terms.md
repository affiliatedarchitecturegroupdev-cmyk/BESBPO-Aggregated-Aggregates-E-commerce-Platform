# Plant Hire & Site Services Terms

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

These terms apply when you request or book plant hire (such as TLBs,
excavators, tippers and rollers) or a site service (such as haulage, rubble
removal, skip bins, site clearing or demolition) through aggregates.store
(the "Platform"). They sit alongside our general
[Terms & Conditions](/legal/terms-and-conditions); where the two differ for
a hire or service booking, these terms apply.

## 1. Who does what

- The Platform is operated by Aggregated Aggregates, a division of Besbpo
  Group (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us").
- For plant hire and site services, **we arrange the job** and a vetted
  independent partner ("the Partner") **carries it out** with its own
  machines, vehicles, operators and crew.
- We quote you, take your payment, send the job to a Partner, keep the
  record of the job and pay the Partner once the job is signed off. Our
  fee for arranging the job is included in the price we quote you.
- The Partner is responsible for its machines, operators and crew, and for
  doing the work safely and competently.

## 2. Requests and quotes

- A request through the Platform is an enquiry, not a booking. Nothing is
  booked or charged until you accept a quote and pay it.
- Our quote is based on a written quote from a Partner for your job. It
  states what is included, the dates, the site and the total price, and
  may state a date until which it is valid.
- Prices are in South African Rand. A quote applies to the job as
  described; changes to the job, site, dates or duration may need a new
  quote.

## 3. What is included in wet hire

Unless your quote says otherwise, plant hire is **wet hire**: the machine
comes with an operator, fuel and the operator's personal protective
equipment.

- A **hire day** is up to 8 machine hours. Extra hours and standing time
  (time the machine is on site but can't work because of the site) are
  charged only if they are set out in your quote or agreed in writing
  before they are worked.
- **Transport to site** (mobilisation, including lowbed transport for
  larger machines) is included in your quote where stated. Sites more than
  100km from the Partner are always quoted individually.
- Monthly and long-term hire, demolition and scheduled waste removal are
  quoted per job.

## 4. Accepting and paying

- You accept a quote in your account on the Platform and pay the quoted
  total by EFT using the booking reference shown. Other payment methods
  will be offered on the Platform as they become available.
- Your booking is confirmed once your payment has cleared. We then offer
  the job to Partners near your site.
- If a quote has a validity date and it passes before you accept, we will
  re-confirm the price with the Partner before you can book.

## 5. Your responsibilities on site

You agree to:

- give safe, legal and practical access to the site for the machine or
  vehicle and its transport;
- tell us in advance about anything that affects the work or safety —
  including underground services, overhead lines, ground conditions,
  slopes, asbestos or other hazardous materials;
- have any permissions, permits or wayleaves the work needs;
- be, or have someone, available on site to meet the crew and to sign off
  the work; and
- not ask the operator to work unsafely or outside the scope of the quote.

Demolition and the removal of hazardous waste need specific competencies,
permits and method statements; we only arrange them where your quote says
so. Asbestos removal is not available through the Platform.

## 6. Matching a Partner

- After payment, we offer your job to Partners with suitable equipment
  near your site, one at a time. Each Partner has a short window to accept.
- We tell you which Partner has accepted. For everyone's protection, we
  don't share your contact details with the Partner, or the Partner's with
  you — please use the messages in your booking.
- If no Partner can take the job on your dates, we will offer you other
  dates or a **full refund** of what you paid for that booking.

## 7. Arrival code and starting the job

- When the crew arrives, open your booking and show them your 6-digit
  arrival code. Entering the code starts the job and confirms the crew is
  on your site.
- Only open the code when the crew is actually with you, and don't share it
  in advance. Opening a new code cancels the previous one.

## 8. During the job

- The Partner records the work — hours, hour-meter readings or loads — on
  daily job cards in your booking, which you can see.
- Messages in your booking are kept as a record of the job. Phone numbers,
  email addresses and links are removed from messages automatically, and
  our staff may review messages where that happens or where there is a
  dispute (see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy)).

## 9. Sign-off and disputes

- When the work is done, sign it off in your booking. You can rate the
  Partner if you wish.
- If something is wrong — work not done, done badly, or not as quoted —
  raise a dispute in your booking **while the job is in progress or within
  48 hours of signing off**. The Partner is not paid while a dispute is open.
- We will look at the job cards, messages and anything else either side
  provides, and decide whether the Partner is paid or you are refunded in
  full or in part. We will explain the outcome in your booking.
- If you don't raise a dispute within 48 hours of signing off, the Partner
  is paid. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Protection
  Act 68 of 2008, where it applies.

## 10. Cancellations and refunds

- **Before you pay:** you can decline a quote at no cost.
- **After you pay, before a Partner accepts:** ask us to cancel and we will
  refund what you paid for that booking in full.
- **After a Partner accepts:** cancellation may involve charges the Partner
  has already incurred (for example, transport already arranged). We will
  tell you the amount before we confirm the cancellation, and refund the
  rest.
- If we or the Partner cancel, you receive a full refund for the part of the
  job not carried out.
- Refunds are paid by EFT to the account you paid from, normally within 10
  business days of being agreed.

## 11. Weather, breakdowns and things outside anyone's control

If work can't go ahead or must stop because of weather, a breakdown,
unsafe site conditions or other circumstances beyond reasonable control,
we will arrange a replacement machine, new dates or a refund for the time
not worked. You are not charged for time lost because the Partner's
machine broke down.

## 12. Damage and loss

- The Partner is responsible for loss or damage caused by its machines,
  operators or crew while carrying out your job, and is required by us to
  hold public-liability insurance.
- You are responsible for loss or damage caused by information you gave
  being wrong or incomplete (for example, unmarked underground services),
  or by the site not being safe or accessible as agreed.
- Report any damage in your booking messages as soon as you notice it.

## 13. Dealing with the Partner directly

Our service depends on jobs being arranged through the Platform. Please do
not arrange further work directly with a Partner you were introduced to
through the Platform. Work arranged outside the Platform is not covered by
these terms, our dispute process or our payment protection.

## 14. Our liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability arising from a
booking is limited to the amount you paid for that booking, and we are not
liable for indirect or consequential loss (such as lost profits or project
delays). Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by
law.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
Questions about them can be sent to legal@aggregates.store, and questions
about a booking to the messages in that booking or sales@aggregates.store.
