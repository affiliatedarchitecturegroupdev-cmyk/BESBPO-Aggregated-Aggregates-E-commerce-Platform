# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Aggregated Aggregates uses cookies and
similar technologies on aaggregates.store and app.aaggregates.store.

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a
website. They allow the Platform to recognise your device and remember
information about your visit.

## 2. Types of cookies we use

- **Strictly necessary cookies**: required for core functionality, such as
  keeping you signed in to your trade account and remembering items in
  your quote request. These cannot be disabled without affecting the
  Platform's operation.
- **Functional cookies and browser storage**: remember preferences such as
  your last-viewed category or unit-of-sale toggle (ton/m³/bag), and the
  products you viewed recently, which we show back to you as "Recently
  viewed". This list is kept only in your browser's local storage and is
  not sent to us; use **Clear** on the list, or clear this site's data in
  your browser, to remove it. The shopping assistant keeps your chat in
  this browser tab's session storage so it survives moving between pages;
  it is deleted when you close the tab or choose **Clear chat**. The same
  session storage notes which banners and product sections you have
  already seen, so each is counted once per visit in our daily totals;
  nothing about you is sent with those counts.
  If you choose a town, or share your location, to see which products a
  partner supplier near you lists, that town (or your location rounded to
  about a kilometre) is kept only in your browser's local storage. It is
  sent with each such lookup but we don't store it; choose **Forget my
  town**, or clear this site's data in your browser, to remove it.
- **Analytics cookies**: help us understand how the Platform is used, so
  we can improve it. These are only set with your consent, where required
  by law.
- **Payment cookies**: set by our payment processors (PayFast, Lulapay,
  PayJustNow) during checkout, governed by their own cookie policies.

## 3. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling
strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Platform — such as
checkout or the trade account dashboard — from working correctly.

## 4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties acting on our behalf (e.g. payment
processors, analytics providers). We do not control these cookies directly
— refer to the relevant third party's own cookie or privacy policy for
more detail.

## 5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will
be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

## 6. Contact

Questions about our use of cookies can be sent to privacy@aaggregates.store.
