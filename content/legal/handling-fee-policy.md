# Handling Fee Policy

This policy complements our [Returns & Refunds Policy](/legal/returns-refunds).
It explains the handling fee that applies when correctly supplied materials
are returned for a reason that is not our fault or the supplier's.

## 1. When the handling fee applies

Manufactured and stocked materials — for example bricks, blocks, pavers,
precast products, reinforcing steel in stock lengths, roof sheets in stock
lengths, roof and structural timber, bagged cement and other packaged
goods — are ordered from the manufacturer or supplier for your order.

If you ask to return materials that were supplied correctly, for example
because:

- you ordered the wrong product, size, grade or quantity;
- your plans or the project changed; or
- you no longer need the materials,

and we agree to the return, a **handling fee of 18%** is charged.

## 2. How the fee is calculated

The handling fee is **18% of the final amount charged for the materials
being returned**, as shown on your invoice — the price you actually paid
for those items after any trade discount, including VAT where VAT is
charged. Delivery fees are not refunded and are not part of this
calculation.

The fee is deducted from the refund (or credit) for the returned materials.
For example, if the materials you return were charged at R10,000.00, the
handling fee is R1,800.00 and R8,200.00 is refunded.

The fee covers the cost of arranging the return with the manufacturer or
supplier, the supplier's own restocking charges, inspecting the materials
and processing the refund.

## 3. Conditions for a return

We can only accept a return where:

- the manufacturer or supplier agrees to take the materials back;
- the materials are unused, undamaged and in the condition in which they
  were delivered (complete packs, pallets or bundles where they were
  delivered that way); and
- you ask for the return in writing at support@aaggregates.store, quoting
  your order number and the items and quantities you want to return.

We will confirm in writing whether the return is accepted and the handling
fee before the materials are returned.

## 4. When no handling fee is charged

There is **no handling fee** where:

- the materials delivered do not match what you ordered;
- the materials are defective or do not conform to the stated standard; or
- the quantity delivered is short of the quantity invoiced.

These cases are dealt with under the
[Returns & Refunds Policy](/legal/returns-refunds) without any charge.

## 5. Materials that cannot be returned

This policy does not make returnable anything the Returns & Refunds Policy
excludes. Bulk and bagged aggregate cannot be returned once dispatched, and
materials cut or made to your specification — such as roof sheets cut to
length and steel cut and bent — cannot be returned once cut or made,
except as that policy sets out.

## 6. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit any right you may have under the Consumer
Protection Act 68 of 2008, to the extent it applies to your purchase.

## 7. Contact

For any return or handling fee query, contact support@aaggregates.store.
