# Returns & Refunds Policy

This policy explains when a product purchased through Aggregated
Aggregates can be returned, exchanged, or refunded.

## 1. Bulk and bagged aggregate — general rule

Because aggregate materials (sub-base gravels, crushed stone, sand,
crusher run, ballast, drainage stone, decorative aggregate, agricultural
lime, and recycled aggregates) are sold and delivered as bulk tipper loads
or bagged product cut to your specified quantity, **products are
non-returnable once dispatched from the partner-supplier location**,
except where:

- The product delivered does not match what you ordered (wrong product or
  grading);
- The product delivered is defective or does not conform to the stated
  SANS/COLTO grading standard; or
- The quantity delivered is materially short of the quantity invoiced.

## 2. Cut-to-length and made-to-order materials

Materials cut or made to your specification are produced for your order
alone, including:

- Roof sheeting cut to the lengths on your cutting list; and
- Reinforcing steel cut and bent to your bar bending schedule.

**These materials cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded once they have
been cut or made**, except where:

- What is delivered does not match the lengths, sizes or shapes on the
  quote you accepted; or
- The material is defective.

Check every length, size and shape on your quote before you accept it. Once
you have accepted, the order cannot be cancelled after cutting has started.

## 3. How to report an issue

Report any delivery discrepancy or quality concern within **48 hours of
delivery** to support@aaggregates.store, including your order number,
photographs of the product and delivery note, and a description of the
issue. Claims raised after this window may not be honoured, given the
practical difficulty of verifying a bulk material claim after it has been
used or exposed to weather.

## 4. Remedies

Where a valid claim is confirmed, we will, at our discretion:

- Arrange a replacement delivery of the correct product or quantity, at no
  additional delivery charge; or
- Issue a partial or full refund to your original payment method or
  account.

## 5. RFQ / quoted orders

Orders placed through our RFQ flow (Volume/Civil Bulk tier orders ≥10m³,
or deliveries beyond 100km) are subject to the specific terms confirmed in
your written quote, which take precedence over this general policy where
they conflict.

## 6. Cancellations before dispatch

You may cancel an order before it has been dispatched from the
partner-supplier location by contacting support@aaggregates.store. Once a
tipper load has been dispatched, it cannot be recalled or cancelled.

## 7. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit any right you may have under the Consumer
Protection Act 68 of 2008, to the extent it applies to your purchase.

## 8. Contact

For any return, refund, or delivery discrepancy query, contact
support@aaggregates.store.
