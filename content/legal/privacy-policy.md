# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Aggregated Aggregates ("we", "us", "our"),
a division of Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd, collects, uses, discloses, and
protects personal information when you use aggregates.store and
app.aggregates.store (the "Platform"), in accordance with the Protection of
Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

## 1. Who we are

Aggregated Aggregates is a trading division of Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd,
operating the Platform for the sale of sub-base, crushed stone, sand, and
related aggregate products. Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party
for personal information processed through the Platform, as defined under
POPIA.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information**: name, email address, phone number, and password
  (or OAuth identifier if you sign in via Google or Microsoft).
- **Trade account information**: company name, registration number, VAT
  number, and standing delivery addresses, where you register a Contractor/
  Trade or Volume/Civil Bulk tier account.
- **Order and delivery information**: products ordered, delivery address,
  delivery distance, and payment confirmation (we do not store full card
  numbers — see Section 6).
- **Communications**: messages sent through our contact form, RFQ/quote
  requests, and related correspondence.
- **Technical information**: IP address, browser type, and usage data
  collected via cookies — see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).

## 3. How we use your information

We process personal information to:

1. Fulfil orders and quote requests, including calculating delivery fees
   and routing orders to the appropriate partner-supplier location.
2. Administer trade accounts, including applying the correct customer tier
   discount and standing supply agreement terms.
3. Communicate with you about your orders, quotes, and account.
4. Comply with tax, invoicing, and other legal obligations (invoices are
   issued under Besbpo Group's company VAT registration).
5. Improve the Platform and prevent fraud or abuse.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal information where necessary to perform a contract
with you (e.g. fulfilling an order), where you have consented (e.g.
marketing communications), where necessary to comply with a legal
obligation, or where necessary for our legitimate business interests,
balanced against your rights under POPIA.

## 5. Sharing your information

We share personal information with:

- **Partner suppliers** in our approved network, to fulfil delivery of
  your order.
- **Delivery partners**, including Besfleet (Besbpo Group's own trucking
  division) and our external tipper-truck delivery partners, to the extent
  necessary to complete delivery.
- **Payment processors** (PayFast, Lulapay, PayJustNow), to process
  payment — see Section 6.
- **Besbpo Group** entities, for group VAT invoicing and administrative
  purposes.
- Service providers who host, maintain, or support the Platform (e.g. our
  hosting and database providers), under contractual confidentiality
  obligations.
- Regulators or authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 6. Payments

Card and payment details are processed directly by our payment partners
(PayFast, Lulapay, PayJustNow) under their own security and privacy
practices. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

## 7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the
purposes described in this policy, including retaining transaction records
for the period required by tax and financial record-keeping legislation in
South Africa.

## 8. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
- Request deletion or destruction of personal information we no longer
  have a lawful basis to retain.
- Object to processing of your personal information in certain
  circumstances.
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

## 9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect
personal information against loss, unauthorised access, and disclosure,
consistent with POPIA's security safeguard requirements. No system is
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Contact us

For any privacy-related query, or to exercise your POPIA rights, contact
our Information Officer at: privacy@aggregates.store.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will
be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
