# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Aggregated Aggregates ("we", "us", "our"),
a division of Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd, collects, uses, discloses, and
protects personal information when you use aaggregates.store and
app.aaggregates.store (the "Platform"), in accordance with the Protection of
Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

## 1. Who we are

Aggregated Aggregates is a trading division of Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd,
operating the Platform for the sale of sub-base, crushed stone, sand,
cement, ready-mix concrete and related products, and for arranging plant
hire and site services carried out by independent partners. Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party
for personal information processed through the Platform, as defined under
POPIA.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information**: name, email address, phone number, and password
  (or an account identifier if you sign in with Google, Microsoft, X,
  Facebook or Instagram).
- **Trade account information**: company name, registration number, VAT
  number, and standing delivery addresses, where you register a Contractor/
  Trade or Volume/Civil Bulk tier account.
- **Company account users**: where a trade account's admin invites
  colleagues — the email address invited, the role and any limit per order
  they set, who invited them and when, and, for orders that need the
  company's approval, who approved or declined them and any note they left.
  The invitation link is emailed once and never stored.
- **Order and delivery information**: products ordered, delivery address,
  delivery distance, and payment confirmation (we do not store full card
  numbers — see Section 6).
- **Communications**: messages sent through our contact form, RFQ/quote
  requests (including any bill of quantities file you upload), and related
  correspondence.
- **Trade credit applications**: the credit limit and terms your company
  asks for, years trading, estimated spend, trade references, an accounts
  contact, who applied and when they accepted the trade credit terms, our
  decision, and the payment position of orders on account.
- **Proof of delivery**: the name of the person who received a delivery,
  their signature, any shortage or damage they noted, and, where recorded, a
  photo of the delivery and the location of the driver's phone when it was
  signed for.
- **Supplier portal users**: for people at our partner suppliers who use
  the supplier portal — their account details, and their answers on the
  orders given to their supplier.
- **Enquiries and bookings**: what you ask for through our plant hire,
  site services, job pack, estimator, design brief and other request forms
  (including the site address, province, dates and any erf number), and, for bookings, the quote you
  accepted, payment reference, job cards recorded by the partner, your
  sign-off, rating, and any dispute.
- **Booking messages**: messages exchanged in a booking between you, the
  partner and our staff (see Section 5A).
- **Partner information**: for businesses that apply to or work with us as
  partners — business and contact details, the people who use the partner
  portal, fleet and availability, documents provided for vetting
  (registration, insurance, operator certificates), bank details for
  payouts, and records of offers, jobs, ratings and disputes.
- **Job applications**: your application, CV and contact details if you
  apply for a vacancy or join our talent pool.
- **Newsletter**: your email address, optional name, audience and province,
  and the dates you consented and confirmed your subscription, if you
  subscribe. You confirm by clicking the link in the email we send when you
  sign up; until you do, we don't send you the newsletter.
- **Email delivery information**: whether an email we sent you was
  delivered, bounced, or reported as spam, so we stop emailing an address
  that can't receive our emails or doesn't want them.
- **Technical information**: IP address, browser type, and usage data
  collected via cookies — see our [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).

## 3. How we use your information

We process personal information to:

1. Fulfil orders and quote requests, including calculating delivery fees
   and routing orders to the appropriate partner-supplier location.
2. Respond to enquiries and run plant hire and site service bookings —
   quoting, taking payment, offering the job to partners, recording the
   work, handling disputes and paying partners.
3. Administer trade accounts, including applying the correct customer tier
   discount and standing supply agreement terms.
4. Communicate with you about your orders, quotes, bookings and account,
   by email and, where you've opted in, by WhatsApp.
5. Consider job applications, and send our newsletter to subscribers who
   asked for it and confirmed their email address.
6. Comply with tax, invoicing, and other legal obligations (invoices are
   issued by Besbpo Group (Pty) Ltd).
7. Improve the Platform and prevent fraud or abuse, including keeping
   bookings on the Platform (see Section 5A).

## 3A. Shopping assistant

The shopping assistant on the Platform is automated: it answers from our
own catalogue, prices and published policies, and does not use any outside
artificial-intelligence service. To answer, it receives your question, the
page you are on and the items in your cart. We do not store or log your
questions, and the conversation is kept only in your browser tab (see the
[Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy)). Please don't enter personal
details in the chat — use the quote, contact or account pages for anything
that needs them.

To improve our range and search, we count the searches made on the Platform
and the views and clicks on our product sections and banners. These are
daily totals only: we do not record who searched or viewed, and we discard
any search that looks like an email address, phone or ID number, or web
address. Lines on a bill of quantities that don't match one of our products
are listed for our team, with the quote request they came from, so we can
consider adding them to the range.

## 3B. Product ratings and reviews

If you rate or review a product, we keep your star rating, any title and
text you write, the product, the date, and — for a written review — which of
your paid orders included the product, so the review can show as a verified
purchase. Your review is published on the product page with your **first
name and the initial of your surname only**; we never publish your email
address, phone number, company or order details. Phone numbers, email
addresses and links in a review are removed automatically before it is
saved. Our staff can see the reviewer's name and email address to moderate
reviews and follow up on problems. You can change or remove your rating or
review at any time from the product page, and we may hide reviews that are
abusive or not about the product.

## 4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal information where necessary to perform a contract
with you (e.g. fulfilling an order), where you have consented (e.g.
marketing communications), where necessary to comply with a legal
obligation, or where necessary for our legitimate business interests,
balanced against your rights under POPIA.

## 5. Sharing your information

We share personal information with:

- **Partner suppliers** in our approved network, to fulfil delivery of
  your order. Through the supplier portal, the supplier loading your order
  sees the products and quantities, the delivery address and province, any
  delivery note on the order, the delivery dates, and who signed for each
  delivery — not your name, email address, phone number or what you paid.
- **Drivers**, through the proof-of-delivery link for a delivery: the order
  number and the products and quantities on it, so the receiver can sign
  for them. The link doesn't show your name, address or contact details.
- **Plant hire and site service partners**, for a booking: the job, dates,
  site address and site notes, and your first name. We don't give partners
  your surname, email address or phone number. Once a partner accepts your
  job, you see the partner's business name — not its contact details.
- **Bellwether Architecture & Engineering (BAE)**, Besbpo Group's
  architecture and engineering practice, for a design brief: the brief and
  your contact details, so BAE can reply, prepare a proposal and do the
  work you appoint it for. When BAE submits plans to a municipality for
  you, the application carries the owner details the municipality requires.
- **Delivery partners**, including Besfleet (Besbpo Group's own trucking
  division) and our external tipper-truck delivery partners, to the extent
  necessary to complete delivery.
- **Payment processors**, such as payment gateways and buy-now-pay-later
  or trade-credit providers, when you choose to pay with them — see
  Section 6.
- **Besbpo Group** entities, for group invoicing and administrative
  purposes.
- Service providers who host, maintain, or support the Platform (e.g. our
  hosting and database providers, and our email and WhatsApp messaging
  providers), under contractual confidentiality obligations. Our emails are
  sent through Resend, an email delivery provider based outside South
  Africa, so the emails we send you — including your email address — are
  processed outside South Africa under Resend's data processing terms. Our
  newsletter list is kept in Listmonk, a newsletter system we run on our own
  hosting.
- Regulators or authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## 5A. Booking messages and review

Messages in a booking, and notes on job cards, are kept as the record of
the job. To protect customers and partners, phone numbers, email
addresses, links and social media handles are removed from them
automatically before they are stored. When that happens, or when a
customer and partner who often worked together stop booking through the
Platform, a note is raised for our staff to review. Staff may also read a
booking's messages to resolve a dispute or a problem with the job. These
reviews are carried out by people, and are not used to make automatic
decisions about you.

## 6. Payments

Card and payment details are processed directly by our third-party payment
partners under their own security and privacy practices. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.

- When you pay by bank (instant EFT), you sign in to your bank on the
  payment partner's or your bank's own page; we never see your banking
  login, only confirmation of the payment.
- When you choose lay-by, we send LayUp the products, amounts and order
  reference it needs to set up your plan; your details on LayUp's side are
  handled under LayUp's own privacy policy. We receive how much of the plan
  has been paid.
- For each payment we keep its amount, method, status and the payment
  partner's reference, as part of the order's financial record.
- A payment link our team sends you shows the order's products and amounts
  (not your address or contact details) to anyone who has it, so please
  don't forward it. Links expire, and our team can cancel one at any time.

## 7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the
purposes described in this policy, including retaining transaction records
for the period required by tax and financial record-keeping legislation in
South Africa. Job applications and CVs are kept for up to 12 months unless
you ask us to delete them sooner. Newsletter details are kept until you
unsubscribe; a newsletter sign-up that is never confirmed is deleted after
30 days. Password-reset and email-confirmation links stop working after
60 minutes and 48 hours respectively. Enquiries and booking records are kept while they're needed
to deliver and support the job, and then for as long as the related
financial records must be kept; you can ask us to delete an enquiry that
didn't lead to a booking. Proof-of-delivery records (including signatures
and photos), trade credit applications and the bill of quantities attached
to a quote request are kept with the order, account or quote they belong
to, for as long as those records are kept.

## 8. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
- Request deletion or destruction of personal information we no longer
  have a lawful basis to retain.
- Object to processing of your personal information in certain
  circumstances.
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

## 9. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect
personal information against loss, unauthorised access, and disclosure,
consistent with POPIA's security safeguard requirements. No system is
completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
