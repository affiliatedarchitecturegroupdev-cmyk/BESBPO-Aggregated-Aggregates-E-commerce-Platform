# Shipping & Delivery Policy

This policy explains how delivery works for orders placed on Aggregated
Aggregates.

## 1. Delivery network

We deliver from our approved partner-supplier network of more than 300
verified suppliers across all nine provinces — we do not hold our own inventory at owned
yards. Your order is fulfilled from whichever approved partner-supplier
location is closest to your delivery address, or best positioned to meet
your requested delivery date.

## 2. Delivery pricing — distance bands

Delivery fees are calculated automatically at checkout based on the
distance from the nearest approved partner-supplier location to your
delivery address, and the size of your load:

| Distance | Fee |
|---|---|
| 0–30km | Included in product price |
| 30–60km | Flat add-on, banded by load size (6m³ / 10m³ / 14m³+) |
| 60–100km | Flat add-on, banded by load size (6m³ / 10m³ / 14m³+) |
| Beyond 100km | Quoted individually via our RFQ flow |

Orders that qualify as Volume/Civil Bulk tier (≥10m³ total) are always
quoted individually, regardless of distance, given the scale and
variability of civil-scale delivery logistics.

## 2A. Distributed delivery premium

We don't hold stock: each material comes from the partner supplier that
stocks it. When one order needs materials from several different kinds of
supplier — for example roof sheets, bricks and plumbing fittings — it has
to be collected from more than one supply point, and each extra collection
costs more to arrange and carry.

- **When it applies:** only to orders of **3 or more different products**
  that come from **more than one supply point**. If one approved partner
  supplier near your site stocks everything in your order, it counts as one
  supply point and no premium applies.
- **How it's worked out:** the delivery fee covers the main supply point
  (the one with the heaviest part of your order). The premium covers the
  others:
  - 2 supply points: R200;
  - 3 supply points: R450;
  - each supply point after that: R225 more;
  - plus R150 per ton, or part of a ton, of heavy goods (aggregates, sand,
    cement, bricks, steel, roofing and timber) collected from the extra
    supply points. Light goods such as fittings, tools and paint carry no
    weight charge.
- **Before you pay:** the cart and checkout show the premium as its own
  line, with the supply points your order is collected from. The amount on
  your order confirmation is final. The cart's figure is an estimate and can
  only come down at checkout, once we know where your site is.
- **Avoiding it:** you can order materials from different supply points
  separately, or ask us to plan a phased delivery (section 4A).
- **Supplier delivery charges:** the premium covers the extra collections
  we arrange. Where a supplier delivers on its own flatbed or crane truck
  (steel, bricks, roofing and building supplies), any charge for that truck
  is still confirmed with you before dispatch, as set out in section 3.

## 3. Delivery carriers

Delivery is carried out by Besfleet, Besbpo Group's own trucking division,
or one of our 15+ external tipper-truck delivery partners — whichever is
best positioned to fulfil your order reliably.

## 4. Delivery windows

Estimated delivery windows are shown at checkout or in your quote. Where
we show a usual lead time for a product category, it is the time from order
confirmation to delivery that our partner suppliers usually need, in working
days. Lead times and delivery windows are estimates, not guaranteed delivery
times. Delivery of bulk tipper loads
can be affected by factors including weather, site access, and load
scheduling at the partner-supplier location. AA 60/120 express orders
have a delivery time commitment, set out in section 7.

## 4A. Phased deliveries and proof of delivery

You can ask for an order to be delivered in stages — for example, materials
for each stage of a build — by planning dated deliveries ("drops") on your
order's tracking page, from the day after you plan them up to a year ahead.
Between them, the drops must deliver everything on the order once. We
confirm each date with the partner supplier and email you as each drop
leaves. Each drop is released once what you've paid covers the materials on
it and on the drops before it, with the delivery fee paid with the first
drop; orders on a trade account aren't held for payment. Once a drop has
left, ask our team to change the remaining dates. Additional drops may carry
a delivery charge, which we agree with you before it applies.

When a delivery arrives, the person receiving it may be asked to sign on the
driver's phone, give their name, and note any shortage or damage; a photo of
the delivery and the phone's location may be recorded too. You can see the
signature and any notes on your order's tracking page. If something was
noted on delivery, our team follows it up with you.

## 5. Site access and offloading

You are responsible for ensuring safe, suitable access for a tipper truck
at your delivery address, including road access, overhead clearance, and a
suitable offloading area. Additional charges may apply where a delivery
attempt fails due to inadequate site access.

## 6. Where we deliver

We deliver across all nine provinces of South Africa, from the approved
partner supplier nearest your site. Our Where We Deliver page
(aaggregates.store/coverage) lists the towns and cities we serve by province.
For a site that isn't listed, or more than 100km from the nearest partner
supplier, we quote the delivery separately before you pay.

## 7. AA 60/120 express delivery

AA 60/120 delivers qualifying materials within 120 minutes, for an express
fee of R1,000 charged on top of the delivery fee.

- **What qualifies:** cement in 25 kg and 50 kg bags; bagged sand and
  stone; bricks; concrete blocks; lintels, damp-proof course and air bricks;
  6 m reinforcing bar, brickforce, tie wire and steel-fixing accessories;
  and roof sheets in standard lengths, roof tiles, flashings, insulation and
  rainwater goods. Every item in the order must qualify, the order must be
  priced at checkout (not quoted), and the load must fit one truck (about
  5 tonnes).
- **Where:** your site's location must be shared at checkout and be within
  30 km (straight line) of the nearest approved partner supplier of every
  material in the order.
- **When:** AA 60/120 orders are taken Monday to Saturday, from 07:00 until
  the 15:00 cut-off (South African time).
- **Payment:** by card or a payment method processed by PayFast, at the
  time of ordering.
- **The delivery clock:** once payment is received, our sales team confirms
  with the partner supplier that your order can be loaded. The supplier then
  has 20 minutes to prepare the load, after which the 120-minute delivery
  clock starts. We email you the time your order is due.
- **If we're late:** if your order is delivered more than 180 minutes after
  the clock starts (more than an hour late), we refund the R1,000 express
  fee in full to the original payment method. The materials and delivery
  fee are not affected.
- **If we can't take it on:** if we can't confirm a supplier in time, we
  tell you, your order goes ahead with standard delivery, and the express
  fee is refunded in full (or taken off your total if you haven't paid
  yet).
- **Site access:** the time commitment depends on the site being accessible
  and someone being there to receive the delivery, as set out in section 5.
