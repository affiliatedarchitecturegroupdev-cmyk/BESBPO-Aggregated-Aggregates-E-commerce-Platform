The data is generated by a build script from the research below; every price

## The sheet model

The owner's guidelines: thicknesses from **0.30 to 0.80 mm**; standard cuts of
**3.0, 3.6, 4.2, 4.8, 6.0 and 6.6 m**; cut to length from **1 m to 14 m**.

- **A product is a profile × thickness × coating** — 15 sheets:
  - IBR (686 mm cover): 0.30 Z100, 0.40 Z150, 0.47 Z150, 0.50 full-hard Z275,
    0.58 Z275, 0.80 Z275 galvanised; 0.47 Zincalume® AZ150; 0.47 and 0.50
    colour-coated.
  - Corrugated (762 mm cover; 610 mm for the 0.30 economy sheet): 0.30 Z100,
    0.40 and 0.47 galvanised, 0.47 Zincalume® AZ150, 0.50 colour-coated.
  - Concealed-fix (clip-lock, ~700 mm cover): 0.53 Zincalume® AZ150.
- **Each standard length is a unit of sale** (`SHEET_3_0M` … `SHEET_6_6M`), so a
  stock-length sheet goes to the cart like any other product.
- **Cut to length is the `METRE` unit — always quoted.** The customer enters a
  cutting list (lengths × quantities) on the sheet's page; the quote line is the
  total metres (rounded up) and the exact cutting list travels in the quote
  notes. It never goes to the cart, and the cart's unit list leaves it out.
- **Cut-to-length is non-refundable once cut** (owner's decision, 11 Oct 2026;
  Returns & Refunds Policy section 2) — said on the sheet page, the cutting-list
  form and in the quote notes. The same applies to cut & bend rebar.
- **Price per sheet = per-metre benchmark × length**, then × 1.08. Retailers sell
  these sheets both by the metre and by length, so the lowest regular per-metre
  price across the matching listings is the benchmark. The per-metre list rate
  is published on the product (`list_price_per_metre`) and gives the
  cut-to-length estimate.
- **"Same product" means same profile, thickness and coating class.** Z100 and
  Z150 listings are never mixed; where a retailer doesn't state the coating, the
  sheet stays quoted.

## Pricing family

Roofing is its own family, **ROOFING**: retail at list, **trade 4%** (approved by
the owner, 11 Oct 2026), volume quoted. Tiles
stay in **MASONRY** (4% approved). Roofing travels on the merchant's flatbed —
no tipper fee — with its own delivery note.

## On the storefront

- **`/roofing`** — the line, priced products, a sheet-choosing guide, and the
  **roof calculator**: a gable or lean-to roof's plan size, pitch and overhangs
  to sheets per slope (a stock length where one fits within 10 cm, otherwise cut
  to length; sheets over 14 m are split with a 200 mm end lap) or tiles (+5%),
  plus ridge capping, underlay, ceiling insulation, gutters and downpipes. Priced
  lines go to the cart; everything goes to one quote with the cutting list.
  Hipped roofs go to a quote with the plan.
- **Sheet pages** — per-metre price, stock lengths in the unit selector, and the
  **cut-to-length form** (cutting list → estimate → quote).
- **Tile pages** use the masonry page with roof wording and a link to the roof
  calculator.
- Shop by Category page 4 (**Roofing**: the three roofing categories and roof
  tiles); a **Roofing** tab in Featured; a **Roofing** build stage (between walls
  and paving) in Shop by Build Stage and on project lists; "Complete the job"
  pairings (finish the sheeted roof, roof it with sheeting or tiles, take the
  rainwater away); the roof calculator in the calculators strip;
  `/products?group=roofing`; search, sitemap and footer.

## Roof & structural timber (CAT-30, CAT-31) — Oct 2026

Added at the owner's request (12 Oct 2026) because of demand from the ad
campaigns, and sold **under roofing**: same `/roofing` page, ROOFING pricing
family, flatbed delivery and Roofing build stage.

- **CAT-30 Roof & Structural Timber — 72 products.** SA pine, a product per
  **section × stress grade × treatment × profile**:
  - Sections (thickness × width) and what they're for:
    38 × 38 (brandering, battens, light framing), 38 × 76 (purlins, studs,
    trusses), 38 × 114 (rafters, floor joists, kappa beams), 38 × 152
    (structural roof beams and rafters), 38 × 228 (heavy roof beams), 50 × 76
    (heavy purlins, supports), 50 × 114, 50 × 152 and 50 × 228 (heavy-duty
    structural and industrial).
  - **S5 or S7** (SANS 1783). S7 is graded to order at most mills.
  - **H2 or H3** CCA (SANS 10005). H2 is the budget choice for timber kept dry
    under the roof; H3 for timber above ground that gets wet.
  - **Sized** (planed to a uniform size) or **rough-sawn** — the two profiles
    structural pine is sold in.
  - Each of the six standard lengths — 3.0, 3.6, 4.2, 4.8, 6.0 and 6.6 m — is
    a unit of sale (`TIMBER_3_0M` … `TIMBER_6_6M`). The sheet units weren't
    reused so order lines, invoices and quotes say "length", not "sheet".
  - Attributes on `Product`: the section in `sectionSize`, plus `timberGrade`,
    `timberTreatment` and `timberFinish` (migration `20261022090000_roof_timber`).
- **CAT-31 Timber Connectors & Accessories — 10 products:** truss hangers,
  joist hangers, truss (hurricane) clips, hoop iron tie-down strap, galvanised
  wire nails (75 and 100 mm), M12 cuphead bolts, M10 coach screws, cut-end
  preservative and fibre-cement fascia/barge board.

### Pricing

The sheet rule applies: a length goes live only where **at least two SA
retailers show a regular price for the same section, grade, hazard class and
length**; list = lowest × 1.08. On 12 Oct 2026 **no timber line met it**:

- Retailers mostly list "CCA treated" or "S5P CCA" without the H class,
  some list "SABS" without a grade, and S7 is graded to order.
- Retailer product pages couldn't be opened from the build environment, so
  only search listings were available.

So **every timber and connector line is quoted** with the supplier for now.
The indicative ranges found are recorded in the catalogue's internal notes
for the sales team (never shown publicly). To publish prices: either a
written supplier price list (preferred), or the owner confirming that
unclassed "CCA treated" structural pine at retail is H2, which would let the
S5 H2 benchmarks go live.
