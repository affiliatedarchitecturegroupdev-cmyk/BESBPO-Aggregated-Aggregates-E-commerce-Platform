# Services & fit-out departments (October 2026)

The range covered the shell of the building but stopped there. There was no
electrical, no tiles or flooring, no toilets, basins or taps, no general
hardware, and no shuttering or props. These five departments close that gap.
They join the building-supplies line, in pricing family **SUPPLIES** like the
first six (`BUILDING_SUPPLIES_CATALOGUE.md`).


## Rules

- **Quote-only.** Every unit has null prices, and its status is "Quote-only —
  no retail benchmark". Prices follow `PRICING_POLICY.md`: two SA retailers,
  list = lowest × 1.08. They are added as benchmarks are gathered.
- **Generic and unbranded.** Make, range, finish and colour are confirmed on
  the quote. Each type and size was checked against the Builders product
  search on 2026-10-19.
- **No duplicates.** Products the store already had stay where they were and
  are linked from the new pages:
  - tile adhesives and grouts: Mortars, Grouts & Admixtures;
  - waste pipe, waste fittings, the 15 mm stopcock, gate and ball valves:
    Water Supply & Soil Pipe;
  - drywall screws: Ceilings;
  - roofing screws, hook bolts and timber connectors: Roofing.
- **Units.** No new units and no units migration. Products use `EACH`,
  `PACK` (tile boxes, flooring packs, fixings), `ROLL` (cable, underlay) and
  `SHEET` (boards).
- **Compliance.**
  - Every electrical and solar product's handling note says installation must
    be done by a registered electrician or installer, who issues a
    Certificate of Compliance (SANS 10142-1).
  - Grid-tied solar must also be registered with the municipality or Eskom.
  - Props and scaffolding notes cite the Construction Regulations (2014).

## Build stages

Two stages were added to `BuildStage`, with migration
`20261101090000_build_stage_services_finishes`. Both sit after Roofing:

- **SERVICES — Electrical & plumbing:** cable, conduit, boards, solar, water
  supply, geysers and tanks.
- **FINISHES — Finishes & fit-out:** ceilings, tiles, floors, bathrooms,
  kitchens, doors, locks and paint.

Shop by Build Stage gives each of them picks. Saving a product to a project
list now files every supplies category under a sensible stage. Before this,
all eleven original supplies categories fell under "Not yet sorted". Shutterboard
and formwork also appear under Surface beds & slabs.

## Decisions to review

- Scaffolding and props are listed **for sale**. Each one notes "Hire instead
  of buying? Ask on the quote", because the plant-hire line has no scaffolding
  partners yet.
- Armoured cable is listed per product and cut to length on the quote. There
  is no per-metre unit for supplies.
- Solar inverters and batteries are listed by size only. Inverter and battery
  compatibility is confirmed on the quote.
