# Partner rate-card request

Send to Garlaws, Besfleet and every other plant-hire and haulage partner.

**Subject:** Written rates for Aggregated Aggregates plant-hire and site-service listings

We list wet-hire plant and site services on the Aggregated Aggregates
platform and would like to include your fleet. Please send written rates (a
signed quote or rate card) for the machines and services you operate:

1. **Per machine and province:** day rate and week rate for wet hire
   (operator, fuel and PPE), on an 8-hour day, plus your excess-hour rate.
2. **Low-bed transport** by distance: 0–30km, 30–60km and 60–100km.
3. **Services:** tipper haulage per load (6m³ and 10m³) and per day with
   driver, rubble removal per load (6m³ and 10m³), skip hire per skip (6m³ and
   12m³), and site clearing per m².
4. Whether each amount **includes VAT**, and the date the rates are valid from.

Your rates are your price to us; the customer price adds our platform fee.
We publish a price only once we hold written rates from at least two
partners in a province — until then every job is quoted.

You can fill in `rate_card_template.csv` (one row per partner × province ×
machine or service) or attach your own rate card and we'll capture it.
